01 / About

Independent expertise, built over two decades.

I am a self-employed translator based in Bicester, UK, working primarily from Spanish and Catalan into English, with French projects by arrangement.

My work covers translation, editing, proofreading and liaison interpreting for end clients and language service agencies. I have worked with Barcelona courts, law firms, accountancy firms, consultancy providers and translation agencies.

I graduated from the University of Exeter with a BA (Combined Honours) in Modern Languages, Spanish and French, in 2003, followed by a BA in Translation and Interpreting at Pompeu Fabra University in Barcelona in 2006.

After two years as a proofreader and editor at Manners Traduccions in Barcelona, I became self-employed in 2008.
