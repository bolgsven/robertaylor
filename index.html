01 · About

Words matter. So does the person translating them.

I am a self-employed translator based in Bicester, UK, with more than 20 years of professional language experience.

I translate from Spanish, Catalan and, on occasion, French into English for end clients and language service agencies. My work also includes editing, proofreading and liaison interpreting.

I graduated from the University of Exeter with a BA (Combined Honours) in Modern Languages, Spanish and French, in 2003, followed by a BA in Translation and Interpreting at Pompeu Fabra University in Barcelona in 2006.

After two years as a proofreader and editor at Manners Traduccions in Barcelona, I became self-employed in 2008. Since then I have worked with clients including Barcelona courts, law firms, accountancy firms, consultancy providers and translation agencies.
