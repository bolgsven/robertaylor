02 / ABOUT ROBERT

An experienced translator. A natural voice in English.

I am a self-employed translator based in Bicester, UK, working primarily from Spanish and Catalan into English, with French projects by arrangement.

My work includes translation, editing, proofreading and liaison interpreting for end clients and language service agencies. Over the years I have worked with Barcelona courts, law firms, accountancy firms, consultancy providers and translation agencies.

I graduated from the University of Exeter with a BA (Combined Honours) in Modern Languages, Spanish and French, in 2003, and from Pompeu Fabra University in Barcelona with a BA in Translation and Interpreting in 2006.

After two years as a proofreader and editor at Manners Traduccions in Barcelona, I became self-employed in 2008.
